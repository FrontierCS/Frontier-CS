Problem
There are $N$ balls numbered from $1$ to $N$, and three baskets numbered from $1$ to $3$. Initially, all $N$ balls are in basket $1$.

Balls can be moved from one basket to another according to the following rules:

When the balls in a basket are arranged in numerical order, the ball in the middle is called the center ball.
If the number of balls is even, the center ball is the one with the larger number between the two middle balls.
When moving a ball from basket $a$ to basket $b$, the center ball of basket $a$ must be moved to basket $b$, and the moved ball must become the center ball of basket $b$.
Using this rule, output the process of moving all $N$ balls from basket $1$ to basket $3$.

Input
The first line contains the number of balls $N$. ($1 \le N \le 30$)

Output
The first line should output the number of moves $M$.
The next $M$ lines should each contain two integers $a$ and $b$, separated by a space. ($1 \le a, b \le 3; a \ne b$) This indicates that the $i$-th operation moves a ball from basket $a$ to basket $b$ according to the problem's rules.

After the output, all balls originally in basket $1$ must be moved to basket $3$.

## Scoring

All feasibility and interaction limits apply before scoring; invalid output receives zero. Points are averaged over test files. For the logarithmic branch, write x for the original minimizing quantity and a for its physical 60-point reference. The improvement factor is r=a/x, and the upper-band award is P=min(100,60+ln(r)). Here ln is natural: r=1 corresponds to 60 points, and on the uncapped logarithmic upper band, r=exp(P-60). Each additional point multiplies the physical improvement factor by e (divides a minimizing objective by e or multiplies a maximizing objective by e, as applicable). The lower-band, zero-objective, and completion rules below still apply.

Q is the number of valid basket moves and R>0 is the stored reference move count. Define the objective x=Q, its physical reference a=A=R, and the lower-band calculation L=60*max(0,(2*R-Q)/R).

```text
P = 100,                         if x = 0
P = L,                           if L <= 60 and x > 0
P = min(100, 60 + ln(r)),      otherwise
```

Zero is tested on the valid original count, cost, or error, before the lower-band test. A zero reference anchor has no positive improvement branch; positive objectives keep their lower-band score. The checker reports P/100.
